About

I'm a sophomore at Rice University double majoring in Computer Science and Cognitive Science — which is a fancy way of saying I like figuring out how systems think, whether that system is a neural network or a person scrolling Instagram at 11pm.

I moved to Houston from Lahore, Pakistan, and I've spent the time since learning to build — web pages, content strategies, ambassador programs, and (very much still in progress) my own front-end skills. I care about work that's both technically sound and genuinely good to look at, which is probably why I ended up somewhere between a CS degree and a social media internship.

Outside of school and work, I'm usually deep in a new skincare rabbit hole, singing along to something in a language I'm still practicing, or picking apart the aesthetics of my favorite films.
